## 0. The epic in one paragraph

The gap analysis (`knowledge/procurement-design-CONTEXT.md` §4) scores **vendor invoicing at
45%**: a standalone POS-style invoice exists with PDF and emails, but it is **not order-linked**.
E1 is the item that closes that — *vendor order to invoice conversion, 45% → 75%, +3.6 weighted
points, the requester's priority 1*. It is **step 1 of the build order**, and it is also the
natural GST order: for goods the invoice should exist at or before dispatch, and an e-way bill
needs the invoice number and value — which is why the dispatch/GRN work (priorities 2a/2b) sits
*behind* it.

## 1. The design contract — five decisions for this step

These are the requester's decisions, **frozen**. Every story below is an instrument of one of
them, and §2 records how far the tree enforces each.

| # | The decision | As written in the design document |
|---|---|---|
| **D1** | **One invoice per vendor order, generated by the vendor at fulfilment.** Lines, quantity, price, HSN, tax and place of supply are **frozen from the order**; the **order ID is stored on the invoice**. | "One invoice per vendor order, generated by the vendor at fulfilment." |
| **D2** | **Hard rule: no invoice for an unfulfilled or cancelled order.** | A refusal, not a warning. |
| **D3** | **Correction after issue: void only before dispatch and before any IRN; afterwards a credit note.** | The seam is *dispatch* **and** *IRN* — both must be known to the system before this rule can be enforced. |
| **D4** | **Leave the existing standalone POS-style invoice path unchanged** (a strangler-fig rule). | Enforced by *not touching it* and by the US-010 regression suite. |
| **D5** | The order gains an **`invoiced`** status set when the invoice is issued. | Note the wording: **when the invoice is *issued***, not when a draft is minted — the tree does the latter (§5, D5 note). |

### The invariant under all five

An invoice must be a **transcript of a sale the order already records** — not a second
computation of it, and not a document that can exist without one. Two sentences in the tree say
the same thing from the code's side, and they are the reason to read this file before changing
the junction:

> "THE ORDER'S OWN MONEY WINS. A line whose money the order stored is COPIED — taxable value, the
> three amounts and the total verbatim — because two channels re-deriving one sale is how they
> come to disagree." — `hhub/invoice/nst-bl-invhapi.lisp:1103-1108`

> "the flag would not know it was invoiced, and a second click would raise a second invoice."
> — the refusal that makes the link load-bearing, `hhub/invoice/nst-bl-invhapi.lisp:1370-1373`

Order ID: **not** a column — the correlation is `CONTEXT_ID` (`:1336`), because `VNUM` is `varchar(20)` and an ORDNUM is 23–27 characters (trap 2, `ord-to-inv-junction` §4.2). 🚨 **"Frozen" is not enforced after issue:** the invoice-line `!update` route is bound (`nst-bl-invitmapi.lisp:274`) and `*invitm-open-header-statuses*` gates only `make`/`delete!` — a value, qty or price on an order-linked invoice can be overwritten through the API at any status (`hhub/invoice/nst-bl-invitm.lisp:30-47`). |
| **D2** no unfulfilled / cancelled invoice | 🟡 **half** | Unfulfilled: `nst-order-header-fulfilled-p` → 409, waived for services by लोप ३ (`:1404-1408`). 🚨 **Cancelled is not checked at all** — the guards are invoiced / service-nature / fulfilled, and nothing consults `is-cancelled` or the terminal `VCN`/`CCN` codes. But **nothing can execute it**: `route-invh-delete` is registered and **deliberately unbound** (`nst-bl-invhapi.lisp:401`, and absent from the eight `register-api-route` calls at `:908-979`), so it reaches neither API nor UI; **no dispatch event exists anywhere** to condition on; **no IRN is recorded or checked**; and there is **no credit-note entity or verb** in the tree. |

| **D5** order shows `invoiced` | 🟡 **shape differs** | There is no new **status value**. `IS_CONVERTED_TO_INVOICE='Y'` + `INVOICE_NUMBER` + `INVOICE_DATE` are written **in the same transaction** as the invoice, one column at a time (`nst-order-invoice-link-mark`, `hhub/order/nst-bl-ordh.lisp:1116`), and `make` forces `'N'` on create while `!update` reserves the three columns so a client cannot forge them. ⚠ **It fires at DRAFT generation, not at issue** — so the flag's true meaning today is "an invoice object exists", and the vendor page labels it `INVOICE <num> (DRAFT)` (`hhub/vendor/dod-ui-ven.lisp:3393`). ### US-012 · The order reference on the invoice · ✅ APPLIED, awaiting rebuild + live test (2026-10-12)

Not on the board — a story this file added, because D1's *"the order ID is stored on the invoice"* did
not hold in any readable form. **Measured first, on the live database:** `DOD_INVOICE_HEADER` has **no
order column** (`CONTEXT_ID`, `VNUM` only), and `CONTEXT_ID` is the **CART's** `uuid:make-v1-uuid`
(`hhub/order/dod-bl-ord.lisp:590`) — the browsing session, not the number anyone quotes. `VNUM` could not
hold it either: `varchar(20)` against a **measured maximum ORDNUM of 27 characters** (497 orders,
2026-10-12).

**Shipped:** `installation/upgrades/nst-dbu-invoice-order-link.lisp` — `ORDER_NUMBER varchar(50)` and
`ORDER_ID mediumint` on the header, `ORDER_ITEM_ID mediumint` on the line, three indexes, all nullable,
registered as `12102026-invoice-order-link`. Slots added in `nst-dal-invh.lisp`, `nst-dal-invitm.lisp`,
`nst-bl-invh.lisp` (mirror list + `render-json` `orderNumber`/`orderId`) and `nst-bl-invitm.lisp`
(`orderItemId`), and the junction supplies them (`nst-bl-invhapi.lisp:1151`, `:1340-1344`).
**Proven offline:** `nst-preflight` PASS; `nst-invoice-mirror-check` **55/55 header and 19/19 item**
slots, no `MISSING-SLOT` (was 53/18). **Applied 2026-10-12 and verified in the live catalog** — three
columns NULL-able, three indexes present, version recorded in `DOD_SCHEMA_MIGRATIONS`.

⚠ **Two things a reader will trip over, both in `PENDING-WORK-CONTEXT.md` §15:** the migration needed
`hhubadmin` to hold **`INDEX` as well as `ALTER`** (separate privileges — the first attempt created the
columns and then died with Error 1142, and `apply-migrations` continued *without recording the version*,
so the run looked successful while 2 of 6 statements had applied); and **the running image still predates
the code**, so a rebuild and restart is what turns this from applied to working.

**No backfill**, by decision — the 10 order-linked invoices (ROW_ID 112–141) are recoverable through
`CONTEXT_ID` → `DOD_ORDER.CONTEXT_ID` and deliberately left NULL, because the standalone path's rows are
not resolvable at all and two look-alike populations would be worse than an honest gap. Still open: the
PDF half, the raw `%Order Number%` token in `gstinvoice2.html`, and the enumerability note on
`ORDER_ITEM_ID` (fine as attribution, never as an address) — §15.

**✅ The PDF half is now DONE too.** `hhub/invoice/nst-ui-ihd.lisp`'s `%Order Number%` substitution is live,
fed by `invoice-order-number-for-print` — which reads the modern `nst-invh` (the legacy `dod-Invoice-Header`
the public page hydrates has no order slot, and one was deliberately NOT added to it because that class
also serves the standalone POS invoice), falls back to `CONTEXT_ID` → `DOD_ORDER` so invoices raised
BEFORE US-012 still print their number, and takes its tenant from the HEADER rather than a session, because
the public page has no vendor session and a session-based read would have printed the number in the
vendor's preview while dropping it from the delivered PDF. Templates: only `gstinvoice2.html` carries the
line, and it printed the raw token until now.

### US-013 · The invoice edit lock · 🟡 STATE SHIPPED, DEFAULT IS THE VENDOR'S, ENFORCEMENT NOT WRITTEN (2026-10-12)

The requester's story: *a vendor should not be able to quietly change an invoice that came from an order*,
with a lock button and a record of who unlocked it and when. **Then revised the same day, and the revision
is the design:** in India people add items *in the nick of time after the order is placed*, so the invoice
stays **open ended** and **a vendor setting decides**; the column stays and any single invoice can still be
locked by hand. An earlier version of this change hardcoded `:invoice-locked "Y"` in the junction and
backfilled the 10 order-linked invoices — **both withdrawn**, the second because it applied a choice no
vendor had made.

`installation/upgrades/nst-dbu-invoice-lock.lisp` (`12102026-invoice-lock`) adds `INVOICE_LOCKED`,
`UNLOCKED_BY_USER_ID`, `UNLOCKED_BY_VENDOR_ID`, `UNLOCKED_AT` — four columns, **no data change**. Four
mirrored slots; `render-json` publishes `invoiceLocked` + the audit triple. Mirror check **59/59**,
`nst-preflight` PASS. The setting is `lock-order-derived-invoices-invoice` under `security-settings`,
shipped **`(enabled nil)`** and read by `invh-lock-order-derived-invoices-p`; **absent means do not lock.**

⚠ **Two measurement-driven facts behind that reader, both in `PENDING-WORK-CONTEXT.md` §17:** `0 of 15`
vendor blobs carry a `security-settings` section at all (so a fixed-path read would have found the key for
nobody — it searches the whole tree instead), and a nested section returns its *section*, so the value is
unwrapped by `invh-settings-boolean` for the `(enabled …)`, `t`/`nil` and `"true"` shapes alike.

🚨 **NO WRITE GUARD CONSULTS IT YET** — `PUT /invoices/{id}/items/{item-id}` still rewrites a locked
invoice, so this is state and audit, **not a control**. That enforcement is the same slice US-003 needs
(its "frozen from the order" is unenforced too), and it belongs in `!update`, refusing with a 409. The
button and its verb are also still to build, and the verb must be the ONLY writer of `INVOICE_LOCKED` or a
client could PUT `invoiceLocked: false` past the lock. Full reasoning, the three decisions taken and the
market-fit note: `PENDING-WORK-CONTEXT.md` §17.

⚠ **And the design finding behind it:** `*invoice-settings*` already carries
`allow-invoice-edit-after-generation: t` and `allow-invoice-deletion: nil` — and **nothing reads either**.
Every reference in `hhub/` is a comment justifying a verb's behaviour. So the lock must not become a THIRD
unenforced setting; the per-document flag is what a guard can actually consult.

### 🚨 Two live measurements that correct earlier claims in this file (2026-10-12)

1. **`ITC_ELIGIBLE` is not the blocker for the buyer's ITC card.** It is `tinyint(1) DEFAULT '1'` and
   **1 on all 45 headers**, so the claim in US-009's Gap 2 that "nothing populates it" is wrong — what is
   empty is **`ITC_AMOUNT`, NULL on every row**, which is exactly the trap
   `knowledge/gst-gstr-compliance-CONTEXT.md` records. The claimable sum is
   `CASE WHEN ITC_ELIGIBLE=1 AND ITC_CLAIMED=0 THEN ITC_AMOUNT` → NULL contributes nothing. Populating
   `ITC_AMOUNT` is US-009's real work.
2. **NINE orders are marked invoiced with no invoice number** (`IS_CONVERTED_TO_INVOICE='Y'`,
   `INVOICE_NUMBER` NULL), so the vendor page renders **`INVOICE ? (DRAFT)`** and hides the *Generate
   Invoice* button. The junction's link write sets all three columns or rolls back, and the invoices that
   did get numbers are the recent ones (10 headers join an order on `CONTEXT_ID`, ROW_ID 112–141) — so
   there are two link-write populations and only one is complete. Ledgered as
   `PENDING-WORK-CONTEXT.md` §16; **not** part of US-012 and not to be folded into it.

## 4. Per-story detail — AC, verdict, evidence, gaps

### US-001 · Generate invoice from a fulfilled order · ✅ DONE

**AC:** *Generate button appears only when order status is fulfilled; invoice prefilled with
buyer, lines, quantities, prices, HSN; only one invoice per vendor order.*

- **The assembly.** `invh-create-from-order` (`hhub/invoice/nst-bl-invhapi.lisp:1375`) —
  predicates → header → one line per vendor line (+ the delivery charge) → the order's link, all
  inside **one `clsql:with-transaction` with an explicit rollback** (`:1424-1432`), so a refusal
  at any step leaves nothing a reader could mistake for a document.
- **Prefill.** Buyer, address, both GSTINs, place of supply and the lines come from the order and
  its customer (`invh-assemble-from-order`, `:1287-1340`). Nothing is re-keyed.
- **One invoice per order.** `nst-order-header-invoiced-p` → a 409 whose sentence names the
  existing invoice and the remedy (`:1400-1403`); the duplicate is also the बहुव्रीहि predicate
  `inv-ord-id-unique?` from Document 4, answered from the order's own link because the table has
  no order column (trap 3).
- **The button.** `hhub/vendor/dod-ui-ven.lisp:3390-3400` — `Generate Invoice` posted in-process
  through `dispatch-route2`, then replaced by `INVOICE <num> (DRAFT)`.
- ⚠ **AC deviation, deliberate:** the button is gated on **not invoiced**, not on *fulfilled*, and
  it sits inside the `venorderfulfilled = "Y"` branch — so a FULFILLED order shows
  `Generate Invoice` and an unfulfilled one shows `Fulfill Order` instead. The refusal path of
  US-002 is therefore reachable by replay/hand-call, not by an ordinary click. A service order
  can also be invoiced with `ORDER_FULFILLED='N'`, which US-001's AC as written does not carve out.

### US-002 · Block invoice for unfulfilled or cancelled orders · 🟡 PARTIAL

**AC:** *UI and API both reject with a clear reason; rejected attempts are logged.*

- **Met:** the refusal is a sentence a vendor can act on, in the domain's own voice —
  *"This order is not fulfilled yet. Goods are invoiced after they are fulfilled … a service order
  can be invoiced without it. Nothing has been written."* (`:1404-1408`). Every Belnap answer
  reaches the vendor page in the domain's words (`vendor-order-invoice-click`,
  `dod-ui-ven.lisp:3131`), and a `:U` keeps its honesty rather than collapsing into `:F`.
- **Gap 1 — cancelled is unchecked.** Add the terminal-status test
  (`order-terminal-status-p` / `is-cancelled`) to the same `cond`. Cheapest correct form: refuse
  on the **order's status**, not on the fulfilment flag, because the cancel paths do not touch
  `ORDER_FULFILLED` (`hhub/order/dod-bl-ord.lisp:343-351`).
- **Gap 2 — the log.** `:audit-level :full` (`:1451`) is carried and read by nothing; the refusal
  builds a sentinel and returns. There is no attempt journal on this path today.

### US-003 · Invoice lines frozen from the order · 🟡 PARTIAL

**AC:** *Qty, price and HSN are read-only on an order-linked invoice; extras such as freight are
separate labelled lines.*

- **Met:** the caller never supplies qty/price/HSN — `invh-line-args-from-order-line` transcribes
  them (`:1098-1160`), and the money is **copied** when the order stored it (the docstring's
  MEASURED case: recomputation gave 120.01 against the order's 120.00).
- **Met:** extras are separate labelled lines. The delivery charge is its own line — the order's
  own delivery line when it has one, else the vendor's `FREIGHT-<vendor-id>` product, charged at
  the **principal goods' rate** (composite supply, §8 CGST) and never at the freight product's own
  SAC rate (`invh-freight-line-args`, `:1252`; `invh-principal-rate-triple`, `:1186`).
- **Gap — read-only is a UI absence, not a domain rule.** No `!update` guard marks a line as
  order-derived, so `PUT /invoices/{id}/items/{item-id}` can change a frozen value. The fix is a
  provenance test on the line (the invoice knows the order through `CONTEXT_ID`), not a new column.

### US-004 · Automatic tax split by place of supply · 🟡 PARTIAL

**AC:** *Same state gives CGST+SGST; different state gives IGST; rate taken from HSN; totals
rounded per GST rules.*

- **Met:** `invh-seller-state-code` (`:1082`) vs `invh-buyer-state-code` (`:1089`) — seller from
  `GST_STATE_CODE` → `STATE` → GSTIN digits, buyer from the order's place-of-supply code → name →
  ship-to state → the customer's GSTIN digits → the customer's state. Both go through
  `nst-gst-state-code-of`, so a state NAME and its CODE for one state answer alike — the fix for
  the live cart bug measured on order 488 (Karnataka→Karnataka taxed as IGST).
- **Met:** rounding is per GST rules through **one** rounder, `nst-line-money`
  (`hhub/core/dod-bl-utl.lisp:1857`), with the inclusive split taking the residual on the second
  half so the parts sum to the gross. `invh-paise` delegates to it rather than keeping a second
  copy (`:1060-1064`).
- **Gap 1 — the silent zero.** The rate comes from `get-gstvalues-for-product` (HSN → rate), and a
  product whose `HSN_CODE` has no `DOD_GST_HSN_CODES` row yields **0% with no refusal** — measured
  on order 492 (Tata salt, `HSN_CODE '000000'`), where the 0-rated line can even become the
  PRINCIPAL line whose rate the freight copies. "Refuse, never zero" is the recorded rule
  (`knowledge/gst-sac-codes-CONTEXT.md`); it is implemented for the SAC side, not for this one.
- **Gap 2 — services are out of scope by decision.** `DOD_GST_SAC_CODES` holds 0 rows and its only
  reader caches code → *description*, so no SAC rate can come out of it
  (`knowledge/gst-sac-codes-CONTEXT.md`, notes only, nothing implemented).

### US-005 · Unique sequential invoice numbers · 🔴 NO CODE

**AC:** *Unique per GSTIN per FY; maximum 16 characters; series resets each FY; no gaps caused by
system errors.*

- 🚨 **The number is not the application's.** A MySQL trigger overwrites `INVNUM` on **every**
  insert: `installation/nstdbtriggers.sql` → `concat('NST', lpad(next_id,5,'0'), '-',
  year(now()))`, `next_id` being the table's own `auto_increment`. The verb's mint is dead code
  (`hhub/invoice/nst-bl-invh.lisp:236`, `NST000<random-token 10>`), and `invoice-general-settings`'
  `invoice-number-format` (`INV-YYYY-MM-{counter}`) is **never consulted for creation**.
  (`PENDING-WORK §1d`; ⚠ `hhubuser` cannot see `INFORMATION_SCHEMA.TRIGGERS`, so the schema looks
  trigger-free.)
- **What that costs against this AC:** the series is row-id-driven (not per GSTIN); its year is
  `year(now())` — a **calendar** year, not the April–March FY the tree already computes elsewhere
  — so "resets each FY" is not true of the stored number; uniqueness is a table guarantee, not a
  per-GSTIN one; and the format is a design, not an implementation.
- **The machinery this story needs already exists and is proven — on the ORDER side.**
  `nst-format-doc-number` with `{prefix}` / `{fy}` / `{ref:N}` / `{counter:N}` tokens,
  the counter table `DOD_DOC_COUNTER`, `DOD_SYS_SECRET.DOC_REF_KEY`, and
  `nst-order-number-for` + `*nst-order-number-format*` (`ORD-{prefix}-{fy}-{ref:6}`,
  `hhub/core/dod-bl-utl.lisp:1412`). The invoice's sibling key `invoice-number-format` is already
  in `*invoice-settings*` (`hhub/invoice/templates/invoicesettings.lisp:32`). See
  `order-adhara-stories-CONTEXT.md` S0b/S0c and `../tools/nst-verify-doc-numbering.lisp`.
- **Two hard constraints the fix must respect:** the trigger is not removable by `hhubuser`, and
  `INVNUM` is `UNIQUE` (reference §8.2) — so a compliant number must be **written past the
  trigger** (or the trigger dropped in a migration by a privileged account) and then **re-read**,
  exactly as the junction already re-reads the stored row (`invh-stored-header-row`, `:1162`).
- **Related wording:** the design document's own GST note — *"an e-way bill needs the invoice
  number and value"* — means priority 2a inherits this story's outcome.

### US-006 · Order shows invoiced status and link · ✅ DONE

**AC:** *Two-way link; order status changes in the same transaction as invoice creation.*

- **Order → invoice:** `nst-order-invoice-link-mark` (`hhub/order/nst-bl-ordh.lisp:1116`) writes
  `IS_CONVERTED_TO_INVOICE` / `INVOICE_NUMBER` / `INVOICE_DATE` **one column at a time** through
  `update-record-from-slot`, so a concurrent write to any other column cannot be clobbered; its
  caller owns the transaction, and it is called from inside the assembly.
- **Atomicity, made load-bearing:** if the link write does not answer `:T` the whole invoice is
  rolled back with *"The order would not know it was invoiced, and a second click would raise a
  second invoice"* (`nst-bl-invhapi.lisp:1368-1373`).
- **Invoice → order:** `CONTEXT_ID` carries the order's own context id (`:1336`); the vendor page
  shows the link and the number (`dod-ui-ven.lisp:3334-3336, 3390-3400`).
- ⚠ **The divergence to decide, not a bug:** the flag is written when the **DRAFT is generated**
  (documented as a decision in `ord-to-inv-junction` §5, "the flag is written when the DRAFT is
  generated, by decision"), while the design's D5 says *issued*. Under D3's split (void before
  issue, credit note after) those are different points in the document's life, and US-007 will
  need them told apart.
- ⚠ Recorded, not fixed: **`restore-order-link`-style un-flagging does not exist** — a vendor who
  abandons a draft leaves the order marked invoiced forever (`ord-to-inv-junction` §5 OPEN).

### US-007 · Void invoice before dispatch · 🔴 BLOCKED

**AC:** *Allowed only before dispatch and before any IRN; reason mandatory; original preserved;
order returns to fulfilled.*

- **What exists:** the deletable-status gate and its legal reasoning
  (`hhub/invoice/nst-bl-invh.lisp:54-59`) — an ISSUED invoice is never deleted, the number stays
  consumed, and `delete!` on a non-DRAFT is refused with a contradiction (`:431`).
- **Four things are missing, and each is a prerequisite rather than a task:**
  1. **No dispatch fact.** `dispatch` exists as a गण verb *name* only. Priority 2a's delivery
     document is what creates the fact; this story cannot be true before it.
  2. **No IRN recorded.** The columns exist (`IRN`, `ACK_NUMBER`, `ACK_DATE`, `UPLOADED_TO_GSTN`
     on `DOD_INVOICE_HEADER`, added by `migration-2026Jan-create-gstupgrade-tables`) and are empty,
     and `generate-irn` is a listed कर्म with no implementation
     (`hhub/core/nst-bl-adhara.lisp:442`).
  3. **No binding.** `route-invh-delete` is registered but unbound — the deliberate decision that
     an id-addressed delete on an invoice is not offered (`nst-bl-invhapi.lisp:401`, `:864`).
  4. **No credit note.** No entity, no verb, no table wiring; the design document's own priority 5.
- **"Order returns to fulfilled"** also needs the un-flag verb US-006 records as absent, plus a
  decision: the flag currently means "an invoice object exists", so returning the order to
  fulfilled means clearing a flag that also guards the one-invoice rule.

### US-008 · Order-linked invoice PDF · 🟡 PARTIAL

**AC:** *PDF contains all mandatory invoice fields; generated from stored data, not re-typed; PO
number, order reference, both GSTINs, HSN and tax split.*

- **Met:** `invoicetemplatefill` (`hhub/invoice/nst-ui-ihd.lisp:1262`) fills invoice
  number/date/status, state code, place of supply, reverse charge, vehicle/transport, both
  addresses, **buyer GSTIN**, the line rows with **HSN/SAC** and the CGST/SGST/IGST columns, the
  GST tax breakdown, totals and total-in-words — all read from stored rows, nothing re-typed.
  Templates are chosen per vendor (`nst-get-vendor-invoicetemplatenum`, `:250`, from the
  `DEFAULTINVOICETEMPLATENUM` print setting, selectable in the settings UI at `:315-316`).
- **Gap 1 — the order reference does not print.** The two substitutions are **commented out**:
  `;; (setf invoicetemplate (regex-replace-all "%Order Number%" …))` at `:1290-1291`. 🚨 And
  `gstinvoice2.html:109` still contains `<strong>Order Number:</strong> %Order Number%`, so a
  vendor who selects **GST Invoice 2** (template number 10, chosen by
  `DEFAULTINVOICETEMPLATENUM` and offered in the dropdown) gets the raw token **printed on the
  customer's invoice**. The other four templates carry no such placeholder.
- **Gap 2 — there is no PO-number field anywhere.** A grep for `PO NUMBER` / `PONO` /
  `PURCHASE_ORDER` across `hhub/` and the migrations returns nothing, so "PO number" cannot be
  rendered from stored data because it is not stored. This is an order-side schema decision, not a
  template edit.
- **Gap 3 — the IRN line.** The design's e-invoice note (US-011) needs the IRN on the tax invoice;
  nothing verifies or prints it.
- ⚠ Traps to respect while editing: `site/public/js/dod.js` is **CRLF** (a rewritten file becomes a
  1023-line diff, `ord-to-inv-junction` §6); `nil-safe-template-substitution` governs the
  `[SOMETHING]`-style fields; and the PDF path is the legacy `nst-ui-ihd.lisp`, shared with the
  standalone invoice — i.e. **US-010's surface**.

### US-009 · See the invoice on my order · 🟡 PARTIAL

**AC:** *Order detail shows invoice number, date, amount and PDF; visible only to that buyer
tenant.*

- **Met — a read-only buyer view exists.** `hhub/invoice/nst-ui-cusinvreg.lisp` +
  `nst-bl-cusinvreg.lisp:11` — `select-invoices-for-buyer` filters `BUYER_ID` **and** `TENANT_ID`,
  so the tenant scoping in the AC holds.
- **Gap 1 — it is not on the order.** The AC says *order detail*. There is no invoice column on
  the customer order page, and the register view `DOD_V_CUSTOMER_INVOICE_REGISTER`
  (`installation/upgrades/nst-dbu-gstupgrades.lisp:13`) exposes **no order / PO column** — so an
  invoice cannot be traced back to the PO that caused it, from either side. The link the AC needs
  is exactly `CONTEXT_ID`, which is already written.
- **Gap 2 — the ITC half reads zero.** `compute-itc-summary` sums `ITC_ELIGIBLE=1 AND
  ITC_CLAIMED=0`; `ITC_ELIGIBLE`/`ITC_AMOUNT`/`ITC_CLAIM_MONTH` exist on `DOD_INVOICE_HEADER` and
  **nothing populates them**, so the buyer's ITC card shows zero however many invoices arrive
  (`knowledge/gst-gstr-compliance-CONTEXT.md` §1-2, whose exact trap this is: schema presence is
  not wiredness). The view is also **B2B-only by construction** (`AND cp.GST_CUSTOMER_TYPE =
  'B2B'`), so a B2C buyer sees nothing at all.
- **Gap 3 — amount and PDF.** The register carries `TOTAL_AMOUNT`; the PDF route
  (`route-invh-download`, 302 → the public pipeline) exists for the vendor side, and the
  buyer-facing download/email is the legacy path (`com-hhub-transaction-send-invoice-email`,
  `:517`).

### US-010 · Legacy POS-style invoice keeps working · 🟡 UNPROVEN

**AC:** *Regression tests on standalone invoice creation, PDF and emails pass after each release.*

- **Unchanged: yes.** The strangler-fig rule holds today — the legacy invoice pages, the
  standalone create, `invoicetemplatefill` and the email path are all still present, and the new
  domain has not been merged into them. The junction deliberately routed *around* it (its own
  template renderer, its own freight line).
- **Tests: none.** `hhub/test/smoke-invoice-api.sh` targets the JSON API (ten bound endpoints, 62/0
  read-only, 81/0 `--write`), and grepping the suites finds no cover for standalone invoice
  creation, the PDF or the emails. Two regressions in this exact area are on record and were found
  only by a human, not by a test: the `select-invoice-header-by-invnum` name collision that took
  the vendor invoice page down, and the `hhub-bl-ent` reorder that unbound `payload`
  (`invoice-api-handoff-CONTEXT.md` §1b). **The lesson is the AC.**
- **A known-broken legacy surface:** the public invoice page 500s for every invoice since at least
  2026-05-23 (`PENDING-WORK §1e`, OPEN, UNVERIFIED) — so "keeps working" is already untrue of one
  legacy entry point, and a regression suite written today would have to record that as a KNOWN
  rather than a pass.

### US-011 · E-invoice applicability flag on vendor profile · 🔴 NO CODE

**AC:** *Profile flag with turnover declaration; if applicable and no IRN recorded, invoice is
marked not valid for ITC and buyer is warned.*

- **Nothing exists.** No flag, no turnover declaration, no `EINV`/`e-invoice` token anywhere in
  `hhub/` or the migrations (grep, 2026-10-12). The vendor profile gained GSTIN, `GST_STATE_CODE`,
  `GST_REGISTRATION_TYPE`, `GST_FILING_FREQUENCY`, `LEGAL_NAME`, `TRADE_NAME`, `PAN_NUMBER`,
  `FY_START_MONTH` (`nst-dbu-gstupgrades.lisp:606-620`) — **not** applicability.
- **The destination already exists:** `DOD_INVOICE_HEADER` carries `IRN`, `ACK_NUMBER`,
  `ACK_DATE`, `UPLOADED_TO_GSTN`, `GSTR1_PERIOD`, `IN_GSTR2B`, `GSTR2B_MATCH_STATUS`,
  `ITC_ELIGIBLE`, `ITC_CLAIMED`, `ITC_CLAIM_MONTH`, `ITC_AMOUNT` — all added by the same migration
  (`:915`) and all empty. `verify-gstin` and `generate-irn` are गण verb names with no
  implementation (`nst-bl-adhara.lisp:442`).
- **The rule to encode** (the design document §3, 2026 sources, verify with a CA): e-invoicing
  applies once aggregate turnover crossed **Rs 5 crore** in any FY since 2017-18; sellers at
  **Rs 10 crore+** must report to the IRP within **30 days** of the invoice date; **an invoice
  without a valid IRN is invalid and the buyer cannot claim ITC on it**; and GSTN's **IMS** lets
  buyers accept / reject / keep pending before GSTR-2B, with no action treated as deemed
  acceptance.
- **The buyer-warning half has no surface BECAUSE US-009's ITC card is empty** — the two stories
  meet here, and neither can be demonstrated alone.

## 6. How these verdicts were reached, and their limits

- **Read, not run.** Every verdict above is from source (`hhub/`, `installation/`), the suites in
  `hhub/test/`, and `MEASURED` notes in the ledgers. **No live session and no database query** was
  available in the session that produced them (2026-10-12), so:
  * "the button shows only when fulfilled" is a reading of `dod-ui-ven.lisp`;
  * "the trigger overwrites INVNUM" is `PENDING-WORK §1d`'s evidence
    (`installation/nstdbtriggers.sql` + an observed overwrite), not a fresh measurement;
  * every *not existing* claim is a grep, which can miss generated code — none of these areas is
    generated.
- **To upgrade a verdict to proven, the gate is:** the story's AC executed against the live image
  with the row counts before and after, in the shape `tools/nst-verify-invoice-from-order.lisp`
  already sets (it drives the real DB and **undoes everything it wrote**). A `US-###` label in the
  suite's output, naming the AC, is what stops the next session re-deriving this table.
- **What would change the tally fastest:** US-005 (one migration + a write-past-the-trigger path,
  on machinery the order side already proves), then US-002's two gaps (a status test and an
  attempt journal), then US-008's order reference (uncomment two substitutions **and** fix
  `gstinvoice2.html`, else the token prints).
